# Master's in Digital Health

This section contains selected academic projects, presentations, and research completed during my Master's in Digital Health at Barcelona Technology School in Spain.

My studies focused on the role of digital technologies in transforming healthcare services, improving patient outcomes, and supporting innovation across the healthcare sector.

## Topics

- Digital Transformation in Healthcare
- Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare
- Digital Health Innovation and Entrepreneurship
- Value-Based Healthcare
- Healthcare Information Technology
- Implementation Science
- Healthcare Management and Leadership
- Legal and Regulatory Aspects of Digital Health

## Selected Projects

This section showcases academic work exploring digital health solutions, healthcare innovation, and the practical implementation of technology in healthcare settings.

Each project includes relevant materials and, where appropriate, a brief description of its objectives, approach, and key findings.

The documents shared here reflect my interest in connecting healthcare knowledge, technology, and management to address real-world healthcare challenges.
